🔍 Analyses SQL & Résultats

📈 Axe 1 : Performance Commerciale

SELECT category, department, ROUND(SUM(sale_price), 2) AS ca, ROUND(AVG(gross_margin / sale_price * 100), 2) AS marge_pct FROM order_items_enriched GROUP BY category, department;
💡Observation : Sweaters Men = 36.3% CA (245K€) | Women = Meilleure marge (55%)
🔥Insight Critique : Croissance explosive +61.44% en février 2026 (112 349€)

🎯 Axe 2 : Acquisition

SELECT traffic_source, ROUND(COUNT(DISTINCT order_id) / COUNT(DISTINCT session_id) * 100, 2) AS conv_pct FROM events LEFT JOIN orders ON user_id GROUP BY traffic_source;
🚀Observation : YouTube/Facebook : 15% conversion vs Email : 9.67%
📈Opportunité : Organic sous-exploité (15.3% conv, seulement 8K sessions)

💎 Axe 4 : Rétention 🚨

SELECT ROUND(COUNT(CASE WHEN nb_achats > 1 THEN 1 END) / COUNT(*) * 100, 2) AS retention_pct FROM client_stats;
🚨ALERTE ROUGE : 94.6% clients ponctuels | 0 client fidèle (5+ achats) | Rétention 5.4% vs 20-40% benchmark
